# 🚕 STREETLINE

My first game! A small top-down driving game made in a single HTML file.
Drive around a city, deliver to the red marker, collect coins, and escape the police.

### ▶️ [Play it live](https://harshjawale15.github.io/streetline/)

---

## 🎮 How to Play

You start on foot in a tiny open city. Get into a car, drive to the **red marker** to finish a delivery, and collect **coins** on the way. Be careful: crashing into cars and people raises your wanted level, and the police will chase you.

| Action | Reward |
|---|---|
| Reach the red marker | **+$250** |
| Pick up a coin | **+$25** |

If your health reaches zero, you respawn and lose **20% of your cash**.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` `A` `S` `D` or Arrow keys | Walk or drive |
| `E` | Enter or leave a car |
| `Shift` or `Space` | Sprint (on foot) or handbrake (in a car) |

On phones and tablets, on-screen buttons appear automatically.

---

## 🚀 Run on Your Computer

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

No installation needed.
